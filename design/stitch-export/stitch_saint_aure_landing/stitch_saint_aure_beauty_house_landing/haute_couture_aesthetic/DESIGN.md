## Brand & Style

This design system reflects an ultra-luxe, high-fashion editorial aesthetic crafted for an exclusive beauty sanctuary. The interface combines the theatrical allure of high-end perfume ateliers with the disciplined sophistication of modern editorial layouts. 

The visual style blends luxury minimalism with tactile warmth:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep espresso-black voids contrasted against warm metallic gold and champagne accents.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Deliberate balance between generous negative space, dramatic scale transitions, and poetic text alignments reminiscent of Vogue or Harper's Bazaar.
- **Subtle Luminescence:** Micro-interactions and card borders that capture specular highlights, suggesting polished obsidian, brushed bronze, and ambient salon glow.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an opulent, low-lit environment:

### Dark Mode (Primary Default)
- **Deep Ground Canvas (`#0B0908`):** The primary canvas, evoking dark velvet and obsidian marble.
- **Surface Elevation / Cards (`#292725`):** A warm, dark graphite-umber providing soft contrast without introducing harsh greys.
- **Surface Alternative (`#1A1715`):** Intermediate layer for section alternation, drawer panels, and secondary container groups.
- **Primary Accent (`#C5A477`):** Warm burnished gold for prominent CTAs, icons, key highlights, and active states.
- **Secondary Champagne (`#E5D1AD`):** Soft lit gold used for delicate borders, overline headings, tags, and badge accents.
- **Primary Typography (`#F2EDE5`):** Off-white bone parchment providing high contrast while avoiding clinical sterile whites.
- **Secondary Typography (`#A89582`):** Muted taupe for meta annotations, descriptions, timestamps, and secondary captions.

### Light Mode (Alternate Theme)
- **Canvas (`#FAF7F2`):** Warm alabaster parchment.
- **Surface / Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure warm white with whisper-thin `#E5D1AD` or `#D4C2A1` borders.
- **Primary Accent (`#9D7847`):** Deep burnished antique bronze ensuring legible contrast ratios on light backdrops.
- **Typography (`#1A1715` & `#5E544A`):** Deep espresso for primary headlines, antique hazel for secondary descriptions.

## Typography

The type system balances classical serif grace with structured modernist precision:

- **Editorial Titles & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for dramatic statements, service titles, and key luxury markers. Uppercase treatments in headlines should use regular weights (`400`) and slight negative kerning on large sizes, maintaining an airy, refined cadence.
- **Body & Meta Information (`DM Sans`):** Clean, geometric grotesque type provides instant clarity and contrasts against the flourishes of the serif headlines.
- **Overlines & Micro-Labels:** Styled in all-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.14em` to `0.22em`) to ground the layout and echo fine fragrance packaging.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive grid governed by intentional asymmetry and generous breathing room:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Outer margin of `3rem` (`48px`) scaling up to `6rem` for ultra-wide hero viewports. Column gutter of `1.5rem` (`24px`). Editorial compositions span unbalanced column sets (e.g., 5-column offset image paired with 6-column narrative card).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Gutter of `1.25rem` (`20px`), margins of `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Outer margin of `1.25rem` (`20px`), gutter of `1rem` (`16px`). Fluid full-width stacking with preserved edge margins.
- **Rhythm:** Generous vertical section padding (`space-xl` multiplied by 2 to 3) ensures each featured service and mood vignette feels solitary and distinguished.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects standard generic drop shadows in favor of ambient luminescence and tonal layering:

- **Tonal Substrates:** Surface containers (`#292725`) sit directly on the deep ground (`#0B0908`), framed by hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(197, 164, 119, 0.25)`).
- **Specular Edge Highlights:** Active and featured cards feature top-to-bottom or radial gold gradients along their perimeter: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(229, 209, 173, 0.45) 0%, rgba(197, 164, 119, 0.1) 40%, rgba(197, 164, 119, 0.3) 100%)`.
- **Golden Glow Atmosphere:** Modal overlays, hero imagery backdrops, and featured cards deploy soft ambient lighting via radial blurs (`filter: blur(48px)`, `background: rgba(197, 164, 119, 0.08)`).
- **Light Theme Depth:** Surfaces employ layered micro-depth (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(35, 25, 15, 0.06)`) paired with warm metallic outlines (`rgba(157, 120, 71, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The geometric architecture uses measured, intentional corner softening:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Set to `12px` (`0.75rem`), balancing sleek architectural rigor with tactile softness.
- **Action Buttons & Badges:** Use full pills (`9999px`) or soft rects (`rounded-lg`) depending on component hierarchy. Primary action buttons default to pill silhouettes to balance the rectangular grid of the cards.
- **Media Containers:** Image frames inherit the card's `12px` radius and include an inner `1px` inner-stroke border (`rgba(229, 209, 173, 0.2)`) to frame photography as bespoke museum portraiture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), subtly backlit with a soft metallic gradient: `linear-gradient(180deg, #E5D1AD 0%, #C5A477 100%)`. Text is styled in dark charcoal (`#0B0908`), uppercase `label-lg`, with `0.14em` letter-spacing. Hover introduces a warm golden outer aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(197, 164, 119, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid #C5A477`, text in `#E5D1AD`. On hover, fills with `rgba(197, 164, 119, 0.12)`.
- **Text Link CTA:** Clean `label-lg` with a hairline underline offset by `6px`, expanding on hover from left to right in `#C5A477`.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Featured Service Card:** Built with `#292725` background, `12px` border radius, and a dual-tone hairline stroke (`#C5A477` / `#E5D1AD`). Incorporates an asymmetric 2-column or split-row structure: high-contrast beauty photography in a soft-cornered frame paired with overline tags, Playfair title, duration/price matrix, and direct booking CTA.
- **Subtle Divider Lines:** When dividing duration and pricing within cards, use a fine vertical rule (`1px solid rgba(197, 164, 119, 0.25)`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Fields:** Dark background (`#1A1715`) with a `1px` border of `rgba(197, 164, 119, 0.3)`. Text in `#F2EDE5`. On focus, border transitions to `#E5D1AD` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #E5D1AD`).
- **Placeholder:** Styled with `#A89582` in `body-sm`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Category Chips:** Uppercase tracking (`overline`), background `rgba(197, 164, 119, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(229, 209, 173, 0.25)`, text `#E5D1AD`. Soft pill or `6px` radius.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Custom Controls:** Circular radios and slim square checkboxes with `1px solid #C5A477`. Checked state fills with a gold gradient and a rich espresso interior pip or checkmark.

### Theme Switcher
- Subtle floating pill or header control toggling between the dominant Dark Noir mode and the Alabaster Editorial mode, accompanied by refined sun/moon stroke icons in antique gold.
